## What Marina Piccola is

Marina Piccola is the working port of Sorrento. It sits below the town centre, under the Villa Comunale gardens, and every ferry from Sorrento leaves from here.

The name trips people up. "Piccola" means small, but this is the main harbour. Marina Grande, the "big" one, is the fishing village. If your ticket or your tour confirmation says Marina Piccola, this is the place.

## What leaves from here

Ferries and hydrofoils to Capri and to Naples run all year, on a reduced timetable in winter. From April to the end of October there are also boats to Positano, Amalfi, Salerno, Ischia and Procida.

Many boat tours meet here as well, in the morning. Operators give a meeting point at the port, sometimes their own office, so read the confirmation. A few private boats board elsewhere, at Piano di Sorrento or Massa Lubrense.

For fares and crossing times to Capri, see [Sorrento to Capri by boat](/amalfi-coast/sorrento/guides/sorrento-to-capri-by-boat).

## How to get down from town

### On foot

A ramp of about a hundred steps drops from Piazza Tasso to the port. Going down takes under ten minutes.

### By lift

A lift runs from the Villa Comunale, the public garden next to the church of San Francesco, down to the beach clubs a few steps from the port. It charges a small fare. Opening hours change with the season and we could not confirm this year's, so do not count on it late in the evening.

### By bus

Local buses link the port with Piazza Tasso and the train station. Tickets cost a little over a euro.

### By taxi

There are taxi ranks at the port, in Piazza Tasso and at the station. Under the last published tariff, from 2021, the minimum fare in Sorrento is €12, plus €1 per bag.

## At the port

Ticket offices for the ferry companies are at the port. You can buy on the day outside high season. In July and August and on holiday weekends, buy online first and exchange the voucher at the window. Be there fifteen minutes before departure.

Next to the port, under the gardens, is a strip of volcanic sand with private beach clubs. A sunbed costs about €20 on average and most clubs have a restaurant.

Two garages in town, Sorrento Parking and Fuorimura, advertise luggage storage and a shuttle down.
