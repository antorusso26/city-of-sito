|---|---|---|---|---|
| Direct Curreri bus | about 75 minutes | about €10–13 | Ask when you book | Most people, most days |
| Private transfer | 55–70 minutes | about €120–165 per car | One case and one cabin bag each | Groups, late flights, lots of bags |
| Alibus + Circumvesuviana train | 15 minutes, then 50–70, plus the change | Alibus €5 plus a few euros | Hard | Travelling light on a budget |
| Alibus + hydrofoil | 35 minutes, then 40, plus the wait | Alibus €5 plus €16–20.50 | One cabin bag free | Arriving by sea |

Checked on 14 November 2026. Two things to know this season. Naples airport is reported closed for runway works for most of November 2026, reopening in December, so check your flight's arrival airport. And the Campania Express tourist train has been suspended since 1 October 2026.

## By direct bus

Curreri Viaggi runs the only direct bus. It leaves the airport at 09:00, 11:00, 13:00, 14:30, 16:30 and 19:30, stops at Pompei and the towns along the coast, and ends at Sorrento train station in Piazza De Curtis about 75 minutes later.

The stop is not where you expect. Leave the terminal, walk straight along the pavement past the taxi rank and the Alibus stop, and turn right at the "bus turistici" sign.

Published fares differ: search results for Curreri's booking page show €13 one way, and a local 2026 guide says €10. Check when you book. Buy online ahead: the company takes bookings up to four hours before departure. For the return, they advise leaving Sorrento at least five hours before your flight.

If you land after 19:30, there is no bus until morning.

## By train

There is no station at the airport. The Alibus runs to Napoli Centrale in about 15 minutes for €5, and you can buy the ticket from the driver.

From there the Circumvesuviana leaves every 30 minutes for Sorrento, from the lower level of the station. Fast trains take 50 minutes and stopping trains about 70. The fare is a few euros: sources quote between €3.50 and €5 and we could not confirm the current tariff. Tickets are sold at the station, not online.

Think about your bags first. It is a commuter train. Seats cannot be booked, there is no luggage space, it gets crowded, and the platforms at Garibaldi have no lifts or escalators.

## By hydrofoil

The Alibus continues to the port at Molo Beverello, about 35 minutes from the airport. Hydrofoils cross to [Marina Piccola](/amalfi-coast/sorrento/guides/marina-piccola-sorrento) in Sorrento in about 40 minutes. On the November 2026 timetable Alilauro charges from €16 and NLG from €20.50, with the first boat around 09:00 and the last about 18:30. One source lists NLG's season as ending in early November, so check who is sailing on your date.

It is the only way to arrive by sea, and the least reliable. Service thins out in winter, and rough seas cancel hydrofoils. One cabin-size bag travels free and a larger one costs a few euros.

## By private transfer

A driver meets you at arrivals and takes you to your door in about an hour. Public listings for a car of up to three people run from about €120 to €165, and for a minivan of up to eight from about €145 to €205. A few international booking sites charge twice that for the same road.

Expect a night supplement of around 20% between 20:00 and 08:00, and ask how long the driver waits if your flight is late. One hour is common.

It costs several times the bus. What you buy is the door-to-door ride, room for the luggage, and a way to Sorrento after the last bus has gone. Every [private transfer to Sorrento](/amalfi-coast/sorrento/transfers) on City Of has a fixed price per vehicle, agreed before you travel.

## Late arrivals and early departures

After 19:30 your choices are the train, a taxi or a transfer. The last Circumvesuviana leaves Naples at about 22:00. After that, only a car will get you to Sorrento.

For an early flight home, the first Curreri bus leaves Sorrento at 06:30. For anything before mid-morning, book a car the day before.

## Driving yourself

The drive is about 50 km: motorway to Castellammare, then the coast road. Allow an hour, more in summer. In Sorrento there is no free parking. Garages charge about €2 to €2.50 an hour or €24 to €27 a day, and the historic centre closes to traffic in the evening and on Sundays.
